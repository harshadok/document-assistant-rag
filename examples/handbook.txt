Northwind Studio — Synthetic Employee Handbook

Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave each calendar year.
Leave requests should be submitted through the staff portal at least 7 days before the planned absence.

Equipment allowance
Employees can claim up to INR 15,000 each year for approved home office equipment.
An itemised invoice and manager approval are required for reimbursement.

Remote work
Employees may work remotely on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Other remote work days require manager approval.

This handbook is fictional and exists only for development and evaluation.
